# Wardrobe & Video Presentation Guide

For enterprise B2B sales and high-level technical meetings, your visual presentation must signal extreme competence and stability without looking overly formal or stiff. 

## 1. The Wardrobe
Research on enterprise sales indicates that the specific brand of your clothing matters far less than fit, cleanliness, and camera-friendly colors.

**The Shirt:**
- **Color:** Solid light blue, white, or light grey. (Light blue is universally flattering on camera and projects calm authority).
- **Pattern:** Strictly solid. Avoid stripes, checks, or houndstooth patterns. They cause a "moiré effect" (strobing/waving distortion) on webcams.
- **Where to Buy:** 
  - *Charles Tyrwhitt* (Excellent quality-to-price ratio, non-iron options are perfect for low maintenance).
  - *Suitsupply* (Slightly more premium, tailored fit).
  - *Banana Republic or J.Crew* (Easily accessible, reliable fit).

**The Tie:**
- **Style:** Simple, non-flashy, matte texture. A simple navy blue or charcoal knit tie works perfectly.
- **Rule of Thumb:** If you are pitching traditional C-suite (Banking/Insurance CROs), wear the tie. If you are pitching AI Safety Leads at Anthropic or OpenAI, **ditch the tie**. Tech labs are notoriously casual; wearing a tie to a meeting with a DeepMind researcher will make you look out of touch. A crisp, open-collar button-down is the uniform of the tech executive.

## 2. The Video Setup
C-Suite executives subconsciously judge the reliability of your software based on the reliability of your video feed.

- **Audio is King:** A fuzzy camera is forgivable; bad audio is not. Invest in a dedicated USB microphone (like a Blue Yeti or Elgato Wave) rather than relying on your laptop mic.
- **Lighting:** Do not sit with a window behind you (you will look like a silhouette). Face the window, or place a ring light/key light directly behind your webcam.
- **Camera Angle:** The webcam should be exactly at eye level. If you are looking down at the camera, you look imposing and aggressive. If you are looking up, you look submissive. Stack books under your laptop if necessary.
- **Background:** Keep it clean and non-distracting. A neat bookshelf or a plain wall is best. Avoid heavily blurred backgrounds if possible, as they can look cheap and artifact heavily when you move.
